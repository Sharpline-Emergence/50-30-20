How the 50/30/20 Rule Works

The 50/30/20 rule, popularized by Senator Elizabeth Warren, splits your after-tax income into three clear buckets:

  • 50% Needs — Essential expenses you cannot avoid (rent, food, bills, insurance)
  • 30% Wants — Lifestyle and non-essentials (dining out, entertainment, travel)
  • 20% Savings — Emergency fund, retirement, investments, or extra debt payments

All income periods are converted to monthly automatically. Results are estimates — consult a qualified financial advisor for personalised guidance.
