Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sharpline ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit 503020.ca (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use our calculator. The income figures you enter are processed entirely in your browser and are never sent to our servers.

We may automatically collect limited technical data through third-party services, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on the Site
  • Referring URLs
  • General geographic location (country/region level)

2. How We Use Your Information

Any data collected is used solely to:

  • Understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it
  • Serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • Monitor Site performance and security

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on the Site. Google AdSense uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our Site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings. You can also opt out through the Digital Advertising Alliance.

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit: google.com/policies/privacy.

4. Third-Party Services

The Site may use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdSense — advertising
  • Google Analytics (if enabled) — Site usage statistics
  • GitHub Pages — Site hosting

5. Do Not Track

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" feature. As there is no universally accepted standard for responding to DNT signals, our Site does not currently alter its behavior in response to DNT signals.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

7. Data Retention

We do not store any personal financial data entered into the calculator. Any technical analytics data collected by third-party services is retained according to their respective policies.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete information held about you. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.
