About the 50/30/20 Rule

The 50/30/20 budgeting framework was popularized by U.S. Senator Elizabeth Warren in her book All Your Worth: The Ultimate Lifetime Money Plan. It offers a straightforward way to think about spending and saving:

  • 50% on Needs — the non-negotiables (housing, groceries, utilities, insurance, minimum debt payments)
  • 30% on Wants — the lifestyle choices (dining out, streaming, hobbies, travel)
  • 20% on Savings — your future (emergency fund, retirement, investments, extra debt payoff)

It's a guideline, not a strict rule — your situation may call for adjustments. But as a starting point, it's one of the most powerful and widely recommended frameworks in personal finance.
