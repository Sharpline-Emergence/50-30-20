The 50/30/20 Rule

The 50/30/20 budgeting framework was popularized by Senator Elizabeth Warren. It offers a straightforward way to think about spending and saving after taxes:

  • 50% Needs — the non-negotiables: housing, groceries, utilities, insurance, minimum debt payments
  • 30% Wants — lifestyle choices: dining out, streaming, hobbies, travel, entertainment
  • 20% Savings — your future: emergency fund, retirement, investments, extra debt payoff

It's a guideline, not a strict rule — your situation may call for adjustments. But as a starting point, it's one of the most powerful frameworks in personal finance.
