About 503020.ca

A free 50/30/20 budget calculator and monthly spending tracker built for US and Canadian households.

What is 503020.ca?

503020.ca is a free personal finance tool built by Sharpline — a small independent team based in British Columbia, Canada. The site provides a simple, fast 50/30/20 budget calculator and monthly spending tracker for people in the US and Canada who want to take control of their finances without complicated apps or paid subscriptions.

The 50/30/20 rule divides your after-tax income into three categories: 50% for needs, 30% for wants, and 20% for savings and debt repayment. It is the most widely recommended personal budgeting framework because of its simplicity and flexibility — three numbers instead of dozens of line items.

Why We Built It

Most budgeting tools are either too complicated, require an account, want access to your bank, or charge a monthly fee. We wanted something different — a tool that gives you your numbers instantly, works for both US and Canadian budgets, and asks nothing in return.

503020.ca is completely free. No sign-up. No email address. No bank connection. No data collection. You enter your income, you see your budget split. That's it.

The site is supported by Google AdSense display advertising, which allows us to keep all tools free indefinitely.

US & Canadian Support

The calculator and tracker work for both US and Canadian budgets. Savings category guidance covers 401(k), Roth IRA and HSA for US users, and RRSP, TFSA and FHSA for Canadian users. The tool supports USD, CAD and multiple other currencies.

We are not financial advisors. The results produced by this tool are estimates for planning purposes only — not financial advice. For complex financial situations, we recommend speaking with a licensed Certified Financial Planner (CFP) in your country.

Privacy

We do not collect, store or sell any personal data. All calculations happen locally in your browser — no data is sent to any server. The only third-party code on this site is Google AdSense for advertising and Google Fonts for typography.
